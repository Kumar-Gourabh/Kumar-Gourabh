### 🧭 Featured: [TeamPilot](https://github.com/Kumar-Gourabh/teampilot)

> Team operations on autopilot for Microsoft Teams.

TeamPilot runs a team's daily follow-up work on a schedule:

- **Standups:** a check-in, a reminder only for the people who haven't replied, and an
  end-of-day summary of what people actually wrote.
- **Timesheets:** one weekly reminder, confirmations counted automatically, nudges only for
  the people still pending.
- **Leave:** read from the messages people already post. Nobody on leave gets chased.
- **Weekly report:** a Friday summary for leadership.
- **Supervisor:** checks every card the others post and flags problems before the team does.

It runs many workspaces from one install, and its guard rails are in code, not prompts.

### 💡 How I like to build

- **Automate the boring, keep humans in charge.** Every job has a dry run, and a manual
  correction always wins over automation.
- **Guard rails in code, not prompts.** AI output is checked before anyone sees it.
- **Systems that watch themselves.** If a bot can fail silently, something should notice first.
